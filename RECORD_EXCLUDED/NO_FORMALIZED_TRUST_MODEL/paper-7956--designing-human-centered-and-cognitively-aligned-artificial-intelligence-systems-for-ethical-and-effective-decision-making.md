  title: Designing Human-Centered and Cognitively Aligned Artificial Intelligence Systems for Ethical and Effective Decision-Making
  authors: Jyoti Kumari, Pradeep Yadav, Chandra Prakash Bhargava
  abstract: The rapid adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) in critical domains such as healthcare, finance, criminal justice,
    and autonomous systems highlights the need for cognitively aligned and human-centered AI design. This chapter presents
    an integrative framework combining cognitive science, human–computer interaction, AI ethics, and decision theory to develop
    AI systems that effectively support human judgment. It explores cognitive compatibility, cognitive load optimization,
    transparency, fairness, and collaborative intelligence using recent research and case studies (2021–2025). Five design
    principles and four analytical tables are introduced to evaluate cognitive alignment, ethical considerations, decision
    support approaches, and implementation readiness. The chapter concludes with future directions in explainability, trust
    calibration, and AI governance, offering practical guidance for building AI systems that enhance rather than replace human
    decision-making.
