  title: Cybersecurity in Smart Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) Manufacturing
  authors: Ahmad Zaland, Syed Saad, Syed Ammad, Kumeel Rasheed
  abstract: Abstract The use of advanced Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) in manufacturing is creating a new wave of
    smart factories. These systems can learn, adapt and make decisions on their own, which leads to major gains in efficiency
    and innovation. However, this great power also brings serious new cybersecurity risks. This chapter discusses the unique
    security challenges of AGI manufacturing. It looks at the dangers of hackers tricking or poisoning the Artificial Intelligence
    (AI)’s decision-making, which could lead to physical damage, production stops or stolen designs. It also discusses weaknesses
    from using old equipment, insecure Internet-connected devices and human error. To fight these threats, the chapter recommends
    strong defence plans. These include building security into the AI from the start, using a ‘never trust, always verify’
    approach (Zero-Trust) and securing all network communications. The chapter also covers the need for emergency response
    plans and the ethical rules that must guide this powerful technology. By understanding and managing these risks, the manufacturing
    industry can safely unlock the benefits of AGI while protecting its people, property and products.
