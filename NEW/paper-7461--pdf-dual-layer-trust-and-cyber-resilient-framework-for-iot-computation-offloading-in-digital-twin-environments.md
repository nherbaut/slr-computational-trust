  title: '[PDF] Dual-layer trust and cyber-resilient framework for IoT computation offloading in digital twin environments'
  authors: Yasir Iqbal, Aftab Ali, Ian Cleland, Naveed Khan, Ali Kashif Bashir
  abstract: The Internet of Things (IoT) is rapidly changing the landscape of sectors like healthcare, smart cities, and more.
    However its deployment faces challenges of scalability, security, and constrained resources. Offloading computations to
    high-resource servers is one of the solution to low-resource issue and helps prolong device battery lifetime. Digital
    Twins (DTs) can act as a high-resource platform to perform computation that also enables real-time smart analysis, predictive
    analytics, and decision-making. However, trusted computation offloading between physical and digital counterparts remains
    critical, especially under adversarial conditions. This paper proposes PROTECT-DT, a novel dual-layer cyber-resilient
    framework for edge-based DTs to strengthen IoT computation offloading. In the first layer, the dynamic trust model computes
    a trust score of each node through Physical Unclonable Function (PUF)-based authentication, incorporating authentication
    success rate, message rates of offloading data, and energy consumption. In the second layer, the trained model on edge-DT
    continuously monitors for runtime detection of IoT-DT-layer cyberattacks on the same offloaded data that Layer 1 uses
    for trust. Our real test-bed evaluation shows offloading latency reduced from 38.27 seconds during attacks to 0.09 seconds,
    while CPU usage dropped from 100% to 20-40%. The scalability is also tested using 50 and 100 virtual IoT devices, demonstrating
    stability under increasing workloads. A feature-rich dataset was created by integrating IoT-MQTT and DT-layer features
    with real-time cyberattacks. The models consistently achieved an accuracy of 0.99, and k-fold cross-validation further
    confirmed reliability with minimal variance. In addition, feature importance analysis highlighted that both MQTT-level
    and DT-specific attributes significantly contributed to attack detection. PROTECT-DT demonstrates effectiveness in securing
    IoT-to-DT offloading, providing lightweight authentication, real-time trust validation, and enhanced resilience against
    cyber threats.
